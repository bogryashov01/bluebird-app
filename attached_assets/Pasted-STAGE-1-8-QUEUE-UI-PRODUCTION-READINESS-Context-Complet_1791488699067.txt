==================================================
3. VERIFY PRODUCT RULES
==================================================

The UI must follow these Bluebird product rules:

PRE-QUEUE:

Before joining, the member must acknowledge:

- pet policy
- flight cancellation risk
- baggage limits

Do not automatically mark these accepted.

QUEUE STATUS:

Do NOT display:

- estimated probability
- chance of winning
- fabricated percentages

Allowed:

- queue position
- queue count
- operational/status messaging
- cancellation disclaimer

SKIP THE LINE:

The product name is:

"Skip the Line Pass"

Do not use "Skip Pass".

Using a Skip the Line Pass is intended to immediately win the flight /
close the queue according to the approved product UX.

Do NOT present it merely as moving the member higher in the queue.

Known product pricing/copy:

- one standalone pass: $2,000
- Plus includes 5 Skip the Line Passes

IMPORTANT:

Do not implement purchase/payment behavior.

There has previously been ambiguity between backend behavior and product
requirements around Skip the Line.

If existing UI conflicts with the known production contract, REPORT the
conflict rather than inventing backend behavior.
